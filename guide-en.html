NoteWake Guide

Write down what you need to remember on the day you need it. When you wake up, NoteWake brings up that day’s notes and can read them aloud. This page explains every feature of the app, simply and briefly.

Getting started

The first launch shows a short intro and a two-question setup. After that the app has four tabs: Home, Alarm, Notes and Settings.

Intro

A short five-page intro shows what the app does. Open it again any time from Settings → Watch the intro again.

How should mornings work?

Three choices: Notification (the day’s notes arrive as one notification), Alarm (rings like an alarm, then shows your notes) or In the app only (no notifications).

Permissions

Notification and alarm permissions are requested during setup. Without them, no morning notification or alarm arrives, and the Alarm tab shows a warning.

Language

English or Turkish. Choose during setup or later in Settings → Language. The briefing is read in the chosen language.

Writing notes

Type a normal sentence in the box at the bottom of Home. NoteWake works out the day, time, repeat and list from the sentence. Recognized words are highlighted and removed from the note text.

Dictate

Tap the microphone and say your note. Tap stop when you finish; the sentence goes through smart typing like a typed one.

Add detailed note

Prefer to pick the day, time, repeat, category, emoji and items one by one? Open the detailed note screen.

Categories

Work, Home, Shopping, Health, Payment and Personal. Usually picked automatically from the words you type.

Shortcut notes

Give a list a shortcut letter (for example “i” = ideas). Typing i new idea on Home adds the text to that list.

Duplicate warning

If you write the same note again for the same day, the app says “You already have this note”. You can still add it.

Smart typing times

Choose which times “morning”, “noon”, “evening” and “night” mean in Settings → Notes & reminders.

Turn off smart typing

Want a note saved exactly as written? Turn smart typing off for that note.

Home

The notes for the selected day live here. The date and a progress ring sit at the top.

Swipe to finish

Swipe a note right: done. Swipe left: moved to tomorrow. Deleted something by mistake? Tap “Undo”.

Long-press menu

Edit, Move to another day, Skip just this day (for repeating notes), Share and Delete.

Calendar strip and Week view

Jump to another day from the calendar at the top; “Back to today” brings you back. The calendar button shows all 7 days of the week in one list.

Ordering

Automatic order puts important notes first, then timed ones. Use “Reorder” to drag notes by the handle.

Morning routine

Things you do every morning (drink water, take medicine, make the bed…) sit on Home as a card you can tick off.

Smart suggestion

If you keep typing the same note every Monday, the app offers to repeat it weekly. Say no and it won’t ask again.

Birthdays

Birthdays from your contacts appear on Home on the day.

Celebrate finishing the day

Finish your last note for a little confetti and a short sound. You can turn it off in Appearance.

Listen

Tap the speaker button to hear today’s briefing any time.

Alarm

Set your wake-up time and other alarms in the Alarm tab. The gear at the top right opens sound, snooze and sleep settings.

Wake-up alarm

Pick the time and the days. Under the time you see how long until it rings, for example “Rings in 7 h 20 min”.New

The phone’s real alarm

The alarm is set like the ones in the iPhone Clock app: it rings on silent, on the Lock Screen and in Focus modes. Requires iOS 26 or later.

“I’m awake”

Stops the alarm and opens NoteWake: the morning screen appears, the briefing is read aloud and the rest of that day’s repeats are cancelled.

“Snooze”

Silences the alarm without opening the app. When the snooze length (5, 10 or 15 min) is up, it rings again with “Still sleeping?”.New

Ring again if I don’t open it

How many times the alarm may ring again: Once, +1, +2 or +3. The last repeat has no Snooze button.

Alarm sound

Choose one of the built-in sounds or add your own sound file. Tap a sound to hear it.

Volume: Phone volume or MAX

Normally the alarm gets louder gradually. With MAX, the phone’s volume goes all the way up while it rings and returns to normal afterwards.

Sunrise screen

When the alarm rings inside the app, the screen brightens from night blue to morning orange over 60 seconds while the sound slowly rises.

Other alarms

Alarms besides your wake-up time, like 9:00 on weekends or a midday pill. Each has a name, time and days, and only a “Close” button.

Ring important notes like an alarm

⭐ important notes with a time ring with the phone’s real alarm, even on silent.

Bedtime reminder

Choose how many hours you want to sleep. The app reminds you 30 minutes before bedtime, based on the next morning’s alarm.

Try alarm, Try notification

Check your settings: open the alarm screen right away, or get a sample notification in 10 seconds.

On iPhones with iOS older than 26, the alarm arrives as a notification and may not be heard on silent. In that case a red warning appears at the top of the Alarm tab.

Morning briefing

Mornings are what NoteWake is for. After the alarm, it goes in this order:

The alarm rings

The real alarm rings at your wake-up time. Tap “Snooze” and it rings again a few minutes later.

You tap “I’m awake”

NoteWake opens and remembers that you’re up today.

The morning screen appears

Weather, your message to yourself, commute time, morning routine, today’s notes, calendar, upcoming important days, news headlines and the quote of the day.

The briefing is read aloud

All of it is read out loud, and you can hear it even when the phone is on silent.

On the Lock Screen all day

Your next note and how many are left show on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Set up the briefing

Sections and order

What to say: greeting, weather, notes, calendar, message, news, quote… Turn each section on or off and change the order.

How should we greet you?

Enter your name and the briefing starts with “Good morning, Alex!”.

Short briefing on weekends

On Saturday and Sunday only the greeting, weather, message, notes and closing are read.

Weather and tips

Pick your city or use your current location. Short tips like “Take an umbrella” or “Dress warmly” are included.

Commute time

Enter home and work addresses to hear the estimated drive time. It does not include live traffic.

News headlines

Add the RSS link of a news site you choose; the first few headlines are read.

Message for tomorrow morning

Leave yourself a note the night before. In the morning it appears as “Your message to yourself” and is read aloud.

Voice

NoteWake picks the most natural voice on your phone. You can choose another voice and speaking speed.

Soft background music

Gentle music plays during the briefing and fades out when it ends.

Realistic voice

Google’s human-like AI voice. Works with your own Google API key; 1 million characters a month are free. Without internet it falls back to the phone’s voice.

Say good morning even with no notes

When off, the morning notification only arrives on days that have notes.

Notes tab

All your notes in one place, in Repeating, Upcoming and Past sections.

Search and category filter

Search notes by text or show just one category.

Lists

Edit notes with items, like a shopping list: add items and tick them off.

Share

Send a note or list to yourself as a message or email, or save it to the iPhone Notes app.

Templates

Prepare lists you use often (holiday packing, weekly groceries…) once, then add them as a note with one tap. Name, emoji and items can be changed.

Reminders

Turn these on or off in Settings → Notes & reminders.

Timed note notifications

Notes with a time get their own reminder at that time.

Early reminder

An extra alert like “30 min before” or “1 day before”.

Evening reminder

At the time you choose, it asks whether you’ve written tomorrow’s notes and tells you how many you have.

Your week

On Sunday evening it tells you how many notes you finished and what you postponed most.

Quiet hours

Reminders that fall within the hours you set are moved to the morning.

My places

Save places like Home or Supermarket. Notes linked to a place arrive as a notification when you get there.

Carry unfinished notes to today

One-time notes left unfinished on earlier days show up in today’s list.

Clean up old finished notes

Deletes old finished notes with one tap to keep your list tidy.

Phone integration

NoteWake works with iPhone widgets, Calendar, the Lock Screen and Siri. Find them all in Settings → Phone integration.

Widgets

Today’s notes

On the Home Screen and Lock Screen. Tap a note to finish it without opening the app (iOS 17 and later). Long-press and choose “Edit Widget” to show one category.

At a glance

Notes left, streak, next note, tomorrow’s wake-up time, quick add and a button to play the briefing.

Info

Quote of the day, weather and notes, commute time, and a smart widget that changes with the time of day.

Lists

Important notes, tomorrow’s list, categories, upcoming days, morning routine and a week view.

More connections

Dynamic Island and Lock Screen

After you wake up, your next note shows in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen.

Show calendar events

Events from your iCloud or Google calendar are added to your morning notes and briefing.

Add timed notes to Calendar

Notes are written to a separate calendar called “NoteWake”. Change the time in Calendar and the note changes too.

Birthdays

Birthdays from your contacts are mentioned in the morning notification, on Home and in the briefing.

Add notes with Siri

Say “Hey Siri, add a morning note”. Siri asks what to note, and the sentence is added to the right day through smart typing.

Alarm with Siri

“Set NoteWake alarm” (Siri asks for the time: “six thirty”, “7:15”, “quarter to seven”), “NoteWake alarm off” and “NoteWake alarm on”.New

Siri commands are set up once in the iPhone Shortcuts app. The Siri page in Settings → Phone integration shows the steps and gives you the links with a Copy button.

Appearance

Color theme

Five themes: Sunrise, Ocean, Forest, Lavender and Graphite.

Light / dark

Light, dark, or automatic to match your phone.

Text size

Small, Normal, Large or Extra large.

Backup and privacy

No account needed. Your notes stay only on your phone.

Automatic iCloud backup

Once a day a backup is saved to your own iCloud. On a new phone, “Restore from iCloud” brings your notes back.

Manual backup

Share or save the backup as text. To restore, use “Paste the backup text”.

Delete all data

Permanently deletes all notes and settings from the phone.

Frequently asked questions

Does the alarm ring when my phone is on silent?

Yes. On an iPhone with iOS 26 or later, once you allow alarms, it rings on silent, on the Lock Screen and in Focus modes. On older iOS the alarm arrives as a notification, so don’t put the phone on silent at night.

The briefing isn’t read after the alarm. What should I do?

Check that “Read notes aloud” is on in Settings → Read aloud & briefing. The briefing starts when you tap “I’m awake” on the alarm; “Snooze” doesn’t start it.

Do I need internet?

No. Notes, the alarm, reminders and the briefing work offline. Weather, news headlines, commute time and the realistic voice need internet; without it they are skipped.

Where are my notes stored?

Only on your phone. If you turn it on, a copy is backed up to your own iCloud account. NoteWake has no servers or accounts.

How do I skip a note for just one day?

Long-press the repeating note and choose “Skip just this day”. It keeps showing on other days.

Why do I need to set up Siri commands myself?

NoteWake connects to Siri through the iPhone Shortcuts app. You set up each command once; the Siri page walks you through it.
