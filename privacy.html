Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

In short

NoteWake needs no account, shows no ads, does not track you and sends no personal data to our servers — because we don’t have any. Your notes and settings are stored only on your phone.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Notes, routines and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Calendar: if you allow it, your events are read on your device to show them in your morning list; they are never sent anywhere.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: adding a note by voice uses Apple’s speech recognition service, handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy terms; your recordings never reach us.
  • Reading aloud uses your phone’s built-in voice, on the device.

Information sent over the internet

Some features call free, public services. These requests never include your name, notes or account details:

  • Weather (Open-Meteo): the coordinates of your chosen city, or your approximate location if “current location” is selected. City search sends the name you type.
  • Commute time (OpenStreetMap Nominatim and OSRM): if you use this feature, the address you search for and the coordinates of your two chosen points.
  • News headlines: if you added your own RSS address to the morning briefing, the headlines from that address are downloaded. The app does not provide any built-in news sources.

These services have their own privacy terms. If you don’t use these features, none of these requests are made.

Backup

If you turn on iCloud backup, your notes are saved to your own iCloud Drive; we have no access to it. With “Share backup”, you decide where the backup goes.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it is safe for users of any age.
