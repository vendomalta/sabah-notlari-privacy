Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

In short

NoteWake needs no account, shows no ads, does not track you and has no servers of its own. Your notes and settings are stored only on your device. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Who we are

NoteWake is developed by Emir Tuncel (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question or request, email vendomalta@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

  • Notes, routines and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see or access them. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Calendar: if you allow access, your events are read on your device to show them in your morning list. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Notifications and alarms are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Reading aloud uses your device’s built-in voice, on the device.
  • Voice notes: adding a note by voice uses Apple’s speech recognition. Depending on your device and settings, audio may be processed by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Your recordings never reach us.

Information sent to third-party services

Some optional features request information from free, public services. These requests never include your name, notes or contact details, but — like any internet request — the service can see your device’s IP address:

  • Weather (Open-Meteo): the coordinates of the city you choose, or of your current location if you select “current location” and allow location access. City search sends the name you type.
  • Commute time (OpenStreetMap Nominatim and OSRM): only if you use this feature — the address you search for and the coordinates of the two places you choose.
  • News headlines: only if you add your own RSS address — the headlines are downloaded directly from that address. The app has no built-in news sources.

These services process the request to return a result and act under their own privacy terms. They may be located outside your country, including outside the European Union. If you don’t use these features, no such requests are made.

Location

Location is used only while the app is open and only if you choose “current location” for weather. You can turn it off at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → NoteWake.

Backup

If you turn on iCloud backup, your notes are saved to your own iCloud Drive under Apple’s terms; we have no access to it. With “Share backup”, you decide where the backup goes.

Retention

We retain no personal data. Data on your device (notes, settings, and cached weather or headlines) stays until you delete it or delete the app.

Legal basis

Where data protection laws such as the EU GDPR or Türkiye’s KVKK apply, any information sent to the services above is sent only when you use the related feature, in order to provide it to you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); access to location, calendar and microphone is based on the permissions you grant, which you can withdraw at any time in iPhone Settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold any of your data, you can exercise full control on your device: edit or delete your notes, revoke permissions in Settings, or delete the app. For any request or question, contact us at the email above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority — for example, the Information and Data Protection Commissioner in Malta, or the authority in your country.

Children

NoteWake is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

No tracking, no selling

NoteWake contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell or share personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
